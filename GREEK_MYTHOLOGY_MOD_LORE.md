## 📖 **THE LEGEND BEGINS**

In the ancient world of Minecraft, the Olympian gods have chosen to bestow their divine power upon mortal players. You are no longer just a simple miner or builder - you are a potential champion of the gods, capable of wielding legendary weapons and gaining divine favor.

The Greek Mythology mod transforms your Minecraft experience into an epic adventure where every action can earn you favor with the 12 Olympian gods. From the mighty Zeus to the mysterious Hades, each deity watches your deeds and rewards those who align with their domains.

## ⚡ **THE BIG THREE - LEGENDARY WEAPONS**

### **Zeus's Lightning Bolt** ⚡
*"The weapon of the King of the Gods himself"*

**Abilities:**
- **Lightning Strike**: Right-click to cast devastating lightning (5 charges, 30s cooldown)
- **Flight Burst**: Shift + right-click for levitation and slow falling
- **Weather Affinity**: Infinite charges during rain/thunder
- **Enhanced Damage**: 15.0 damage with netherite armor penetration
- **Fireproof**: Cannot be destroyed by fire or lava

**Lore**: Forged in the heart of Mount Olympus, Zeus's Lightning Bolt is the most feared weapon in all of Greek mythology. Only those who prove themselves worthy of the King of the Gods may wield its power.

### **Poseidon's Trident** 🌊
*"The weapon that commands the seas"*

**Abilities:**
- **Tidal Waves**: Right-click creates expanding waves that push entities away
- **Water Projectiles**: Right-click at target shoots water blasts for ranged combat
- **Storm at Sea**: Shift + right-click creates storms with lightning near water
- **Water Affinity**: Infinite abilities when near or in water (5-block radius)
- **Enhanced Damage**: 8.0 damage with water-based combat

**Lore**: Crafted from the depths of the ocean, Poseidon's Trident represents the raw power of the sea. Those who wield it can command water itself and summon the fury of the ocean.

### **Hades' Scythe** 💀
*"The weapon that harvests souls"*

**Abilities:**
- **Soul Harvest**: Right-click at target area harvests souls with Wither III effect
- **Death Mist**: Right-click in open air creates expanding death cloud
- **Underworld Portal**: Shift + right-click creates portal effects with night vision
- **Nether Affinity**: Infinite abilities in the Nether or near lava (8-block radius)
- **Enhanced Damage**: 16.0 damage with Wither effect on hits

**Lore**: Forged in the depths of the Underworld, Hades' Scythe is the ultimate weapon of death and soul manipulation. Only those who understand the balance between life and death may wield its dark power.

## 🏛️ **THE FAVOR SYSTEM - DIVINE RECOGNITION**

### **How It Works**
Every action you take in the world is observed by the Olympian gods. Your deeds can earn you favor or disfavor with each deity, unlocking powerful abilities and rewards.

### **The 12 Olympian Gods**

#### **⚡ THE BIG THREE (Most Powerful)**
- **Zeus** - King of the Gods: Lightning, weather, leadership
- **Poseidon** - Lord of the Seas: Water, horses, earthquakes  
- **Hades** - Lord of the Underworld: Death, riches, the dead

#### **🏺 THE OTHER OLYMPIANS**
- **Athena** - Goddess of Wisdom: Wisdom, warfare, crafts
- **Ares** - God of War: War, violence, courage
- **Apollo** - God of the Sun: Sun, music, healing, prophecy
- **Artemis** - Goddess of the Moon: Moon, hunting, wilderness
- **Hermes** - God of Travel: Travel, commerce, messages
- **Hephaestus** - God of Fire: Fire, smithing, crafts
- **Aphrodite** - Goddess of Love: Love, beauty, desire
- **Demeter** - Goddess of Agriculture: Agriculture, harvest, fertility
- **Dionysus** - God of Wine: Wine, revelry, theater

### **Favor Tiers**
- **🔴 HOSTILE** (-1000 to -500): The god actively opposes you
- **⚪ NEUTRAL** (-499 to 499): The god is indifferent to your actions
- **🟡 RESPECTED** (500 to 999): The god recognizes your worth
- **🟢 CHOSEN** (1000 to 1499): The god has chosen you as their champion
- **🟣 CHAMPION** (1500 to 2000): You are the god's ultimate champion
