# Greek Mythology Mod v1.0.80

🏛️ **Bring the power of the Olympian gods to your Minecraft world!**

## 📖 Overview

The Greek Mythology Mod adds legendary weapons and divine powers inspired by ancient Greek mythology. Wield the weapons of Zeus, Poseidon, and Hades while managing favor with the 12 Olympian gods.

## ⚡ Legendary Weapons

### 💀 **Hades Scythe - Lord of the Underworld**
- **Base Damage:** 4.0 (scales with consumed souls)
- **Soul System:** Consume soul items to permanently increase damage (max 12 souls = +12 damage)
- **Live Damage Display:** Tooltip shows exact combat damage in real-time
- **Special Abilities:**
  - Right-click: Harvest souls from nearby entities (10-block radius)
  - Sneak + Right-click: Open Underworld portal (requires Inferno Pearl)
  - Critical hits apply Wither II and transform skeletons into pets
- **NEW:** `/soul clear` command to reset soul count for testing/balance

### ⚡ **Zeus Bolt - King of the Gods**
- **Damage:** 15.0 (penetrates netherite armor)
- **Charges:** 5 uses, 30-second cooldown when empty
- **Special Abilities:**
  - Right-click: Cast lightning strikes at targets
  - Sneak + Right-click: Flight burst (levitation effect)
  - Infinite charges during rain/thunderstorms
  - Fireproof: Cannot be destroyed by fire or lava

### 🌊 **Poseidon Trident - Lord of the Seas**
- **Damage:** 8.0 (enhanced underwater)
- **Charges:** 3 uses, 20-second cooldown when empty
- **Special Abilities:**
  - Right-click: Control water and create tidal waves
  - Sneak + Right-click: Summon storms at sea
  - Infinite charges near water or in rain
  - Grants water breathing and night vision underwater

## 🔥 **Inferno Pearls**
- **NEW:** Fixed throwing mechanics - now work exactly like ender pearls!
- Corrupt areas with Nether blocks when thrown
- Transform regular portals near Nether portals
- Only usable in the Overworld
- Visual trajectory and proper physics

## 🏛️ **Favor System**

Gain favor with the 12 Olympian gods through your actions:

### ⚡ **The Big Three** (Most Powerful)
- **Zeus:** Lightning, weather, leadership
- **Poseidon:** Water, horses, earthquakes  
- **Hades:** Death, riches, the underworld

### 🏺 **Other Olympians**
- **Athena:** Wisdom, warfare, crafts
- **Ares:** War, violence, courage
- **Apollo:** Sun, music, healing, prophecy
- **Artemis:** Moon, hunting, wilderness
- **Hermes:** Travel, commerce, messages
- **Hephaestus:** Fire, smithing, crafts
- **Aphrodite:** Love, beauty, desire
- **Demeter:** Agriculture, harvest, fertility
- **Dionysus:** Wine, revelry, theater

## 💬 **Commands**

- `/help greekmyth` - Show comprehensive help
- `/favor get <GOD>` - Check favor with specific god
- `/favor profile` - View complete favor profile
- `/soul clear` - **NEW!** Reset Hades Scythe soul count to 0
- `/jail <player> <cell>` - Admin jail system (OP only)
- `/escape` - Escape from jail

## 🔧 **Installation**

### Requirements
- **Minecraft:** 1.21.8
- **Fabric Loader:** Latest version
- **Fabric API:** 0.129.0 or newer
- **Java:** 17 or higher

### Steps
1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft 1.21.8
2. Download [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api)
3. Place both `fabric-api-x.x.x.jar` and `greek-mythology-1.0.80.jar` in your `mods` folder
4. Launch Minecraft with the Fabric profile
5. Enjoy the power of the gods!

## 🎮 **Getting Started**

1. **Creative Mode:** Find Greek Mythology items in the creative inventory
2. **Survival Mode:** Craft or use commands to obtain legendary weapons
3. **Test Features:** Use `/soul clear` to reset Hades Scythe for testing
4. **Build Favor:** Perform actions aligned with each god's domain
5. **Master Combat:** Learn each weapon's unique abilities and combos
